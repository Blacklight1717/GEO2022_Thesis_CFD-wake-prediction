#### Turbulence Models

- Recommendations from wind engineering literature suggest that standard k-epsilon can suffer from excessive turbulent kinetic energy production.

- Realizable k-epsilon can show unrealistic acceleration in some cases.
  - A large number of cases had to be discarded because of this behaviour.
  - Similar issues may occur with RNG k-epsilon.
  - Instead of relying solely on references, this can be explained through the non-equilibrium nature of the wall functions and the formulation of the realizable k-epsilon model.

- Standard k-epsilon is generally acceptable.

- k-omega models need to be at y+=1 to perform properly (very fine mesh).
  - We likely cannot achieve this.
  - Check whether suitable wall functions exist.
  - Run both k-omega and k-epsilon for comparison.
  - Industry often uses k-omega models.

- SST k-omega was discussed as an alternative formulation.

#### LES / DES

- Blocken describes LES as less common than RANS approaches.
- DES is not very common.
- The gains compared to LES may not justify the additional complexity.

- LES quality depends on the ability to resolve a sufficient proportion of the turbulent structures.
  - Need to resolve approximately 80% of the eddies for a good LES case.
  - Further reading:
    - Blocken paper.
    - Pope turbulence book (E and λ discussion).

- It may be worthwhile to run one or two LES cases on the full domain since the objective is to evaluate turbulence across the entire measurement line, instead of many cases with cut down domains
