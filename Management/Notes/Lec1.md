# How do I do research?

## finding articles
- [ ] google scholar for recent papers
- [ ] find a recent review paper and/or a PhD/MSc thesis
- [ ] scihub (on telegram)
- [ ] and also be very critical because a lot of research papers are crap 🤷‍♀️, you should know better than just accept what is written as true

## managing your articles
- [ ] jabref/mendeley/bibdesk (macOs) and write down summary in your own words
  - drop a DOI
  - doi2bib.org
- [ ] keep your notes on paper/iPad
- [ ] assign them a key that you keep

## writing 
- [ ] read papers and msc theses. If you don't read you won't be able to write
- [ ] Don't use Word. Friends don't let friends use Word for their thesis.
- [ ] put your source Typst/LaTeX on Git (you won’t regret it)
  - one sentence per line!
  - https://3d.bk.tudelft.nl/hledoux/blog/rules-happy-latex/
- [ ] write summaries every week, those will be useful at the end
- [ ] I still use paper to think and write, I really do
- [ ] Stuck? the *vomit draft* tip
- [ ] I don’t write well under-pressure, I aim at finishing 2w before a deadline
- [ ] figures: make an effort to have them vector (IPE)

## LLMs
- [ ] LLMs are really good at finding inconsistencies in LaTeX/Typst and harmonising your text
- [ ] use git (locally also good) to keep track of what the LLM is modifying, if you don't ==> hello hallucinated references
- [ ] don't ever add a new entry to your bibtex without manually checking that it exist, and open the paper and skim it (bare mininum)

## code
- [ ] keep it structured, for you and for your supervisors and others
- [ ] use Git(Hub)
- [ ] at the end, add a README.md, license, versions, etc. you can follow the checklist on geogeeks

## misc
- [ ] Stuck? Go for a walk or bike ride
- [ ] it’s not a sprint, it’s a marathon
- [ ] If it feels daunting and tiresome: you’re not alone, it’s meant to be this way 😬
