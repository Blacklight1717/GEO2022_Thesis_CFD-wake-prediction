# Lecture 2 - Referencing
## References
- Reports have chapters, sections, subsections, subsubsections, etc...

- you can write either by first mapping out the entire structure, or figuring it out along the way.

- chapters for the thesis can roughly be divided into:
    - Introduction
    - Theory/literature review
    - Methodology
    - Results
    - Limitations
    - Discussion/conclusion
    - Future research/recommendations

- There is a natural progression between these chapters.

- Dont be stupid and use internal references for sources, figures, tables, chapters, etc.
